CxO Open Mic brings intelligence into conversation—and carries what we learn forward. Three connected products support the experience before, during, and after each gathering.

At the centre / Open Mic

The room brings it to life.

In CISO Open Mic and CAIO Open Mic, leaders bring lived experience, challenge assumptions, and share practical lessons. Five minutes. No decks. No sales pitches.

Radar supplies context. Amplify offers optional prompts. Speakers bring their own topics and perspectives. Resonance develops permission-cleared learning from the conversation.
