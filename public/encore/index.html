How does Encore fit with Draft Nights and the main event?

Draft Nights help develop ideas and connections. The main event brings the community together through short practitioner talks. Encore takes selected conversations deeper through focused follow-up gatherings.

How does Encore relate to Radar, Amplify, and Resonance?

Radar provides context, Amplify develops briefs and prompts, and Resonance carries learning forward. Encore is a way to bring people together again around a focused topic within the Open Mic event family.

Who is Encore for?

Leaders who want to continue a relevant Open Mic conversation, share practical experience, and explore a specific challenge with peers. CISO Open Mic Encore brings a security leadership perspective to that exchange.

How can I take part or suggest a topic?

Contact the CxO Open Mic team with your city, the topic you would like to explore, and your interest in a dinner, roundtable, or summit.
